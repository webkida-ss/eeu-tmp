## Distribution Evidence

Readlang connects its reading experience with a browser web reader and points visitors to mobile access. LingQ exposes language-specific pages, learning resources, an affiliate program, and school/business/library entry points. These are observable acquisition surfaces; this research does not establish their conversion performance. [Readlang](https://readlang.com/), [LingQ](https://www.lingq.com/en/signup/)

Immersive Translate publishes use-case pages spanning documents, images, subtitles and websites, and distributes across desktop browsers and mobile. Its pricing FAQ describes an affiliate channel with 20% commission when a referral upgrades within 60 days and a 5% first-period discount. These terms demonstrate a distribution mechanism, not a suitable default budget for Untangle. ## Implications to Test, Not Established Market Facts

1. **Choose a narrower recurring problem.** General translation and contextual explanation have established alternatives. Test whether Japanese-speaking professionals who regularly read difficult English need sentence-structure understanding strongly enough to pay, rather than assuming a generic AI-reading pitch wins.
2. **Demonstrate a measurable outcome.** Compare completion and comprehension of a difficult paragraph with participants' current tools. A structure display is only valuable if it changes the reading outcome or repeat usage.
3. **Price against willingness to pay and service cost.** Public subscriptions provide reference points, not a statistically valid market price. Test one clear paid offer with recruited users before multiplying tiers. Avoid promising unlimited model-backed usage without measured cost distributions.
4. **Keep the first successful session easy.** Test a free allowance sufficient to experience the core workflow. Charge for sustained repeated use or demonstrated additional value. Do not copy a competitor's quota without observing Untangle activation and abuse costs.
5. **Start distribution where the problem is visible.** Test short demonstrations on representative public technical articles, a focused landing page, and voluntarily recruited users from relevant professional communities. Treat creator partnerships and affiliates as later experiments once retention and contribution margin support acquisition spending.
6. **Validate the competitive claim directly.** Include Readlang and the user's existing translator in interviews and comparative tasks. This research does not prove competitors lack structural explanations, nor that users will switch tools for them.

No customer interviews, paid campaigns, extension installation, subscription purchase, authentication, or external publication occurred during this research. The next evidence needed is actual user behavior, cohort retention, willingness to pay, and measured delivery cost; public marketing pages cannot supply those inputs.
